Hydration
Non-Negotiable
1 gallon water/day. 2 LMNT packs (or salt + lemon) — one before workout, one at noon. Keto + TBI + sweat = electrolyte crashes if you skip this.
Crohn's Note
Start with cooked spinach, zucchini, asparagus the first 2 weeks. Hold off on raw broccoli/cauliflower/cabbage until your gut adapts to keto. Salmon, ribeye, ground beef are all gut-safe.

The Rules
Read Before Day 1
01
Electrolytes before EVERY fasted workout. LMNT or salt + lemon 30 min before. With TBI, skipping this = dizzy spells. Non-negotiable.
02
Lift first, cardio second. Don't flip the order — cardio before lifts tanks strength output.
03
Last 2 reps should feel hard. Not impossible. If reps 1-10 feel easy, the weight is too light.
04
HIIT speeds adjust. Week 1 if brutal, drop 0.5 mph. If easy, add 0.5 mph. The chart is a starting point, not the law.
05
Track 3 things daily: AM weight (same time, post-bathroom), workout completed Y/N, energy 1-10. Adjust calories down 200 if scale stalls past day 10.
06
Lightheaded = stop. Pinch of salt under tongue, sip water, sit. TBI + low blood sugar + intervals is a real risk. Don't push through.
07
Don't weigh daily and freak. Keto water shifts are wild for the first 2 weeks. Track the trend, not the number.
08
One Barry's studio class/week. Wed Chest/Back/Abs. Energy + accountability + heavier weights. Worth it.
09
Saturday = real recovery. Don't sneak in a "light lift." The walk is the workout. Your CNS needs it.
10
Stop eating by 8pm. 16-hour fast before next session. Lets your gut settle and burns fat overnight.
